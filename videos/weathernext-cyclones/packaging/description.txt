THE NUMBERS
- +24 hours of lead time: WeatherNext's 3-day forecasts are as accurate as the best 2-day forecasts - about a decade of progress in one model
- Hurricane Melissa (2025): Jamaica landfall as a Category 5 - 80% confidence five days out, near-certain three days out; first-ever NHC Category-5 call from a Category-1 start
- NHC 2025 Verification Report: the top-performing individual model on track AND intensity; outperformed the NHC's official forecasts at 12-72h; beat every model and consensus in the eastern Pacific at 48-120h
- 3-day track error about 100 km - better than the European model; intensity error about 11 knots - beats the US Navy's hurricane model
- Forecasts out to 15 days; 1,000-member ensembles per storm (last year it was 50); a full 15-day run in under a minute on a single TPU
- Inputs are just 28 x 28 km - about 100x coarser than traditional models, and it still performs
- The stakes: 700,000+ deaths and $1.4 trillion in damage from cyclones over the last 50 years
- Open source: code + weights on GitHub, with a mini model that runs free in a Colab notebook
