Same interest rate. Same loan. Same house. So why did your mortgage payment go up anyway?

The answer is an escrow account — the part of your payment that covers property tax and homeowners insurance on your behalf. Per Insurify, insurance rose 12% in 2025 alone. Per ATTOM Data Solutions, property tax rose 3% even as home values dipped. Add them together, divide by twelve, and that gap is the letter in your mailbox.
