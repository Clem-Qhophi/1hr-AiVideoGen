# Title

**Locked title:** The AI Kill Switch Act: Why Every Tech CEO Is Scared

**Implied expectation:** A real bill in Congress could force AI companies to build shutdown mechanisms into their models. The viewer learns what the bill does, who triggered it, and what it means for anyone who uses AI.

**Title checklist self-check:**
1. Concrete promise — names the specific bill, concrete mechanism (shutdown)
2. Magnet word — "Kill Switch" is the magnet
3. Outcome-framed — "Why Every Tech CEO Is Scared" = emotional stakes for the viewer
4. No unknown names — "AI Kill Switch Act" is searchable
5. No barrier-drop needed — broad audience
6. News hook — "Act" = legislation, current event
7. Specific noun — the bill's name
8. No DEAD/negative framing — "scared" is emotion, not "AI is dead"
9. Leads with the takeaway — the bill name is the headline

**Alternate titles considered:**
- "AI Companies Are Begging for a Kill Switch — That's the Scary Part" (curiosity)
- "The Kill Switch Bill Just Changed AI Forever" (declarative)

**Final choice: "The AI Kill Switch Act: Why Every Tech CEO Is Scared"** — names the bill directly, curiosity hook on the second half, search-friendly.
