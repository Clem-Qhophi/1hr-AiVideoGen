There's a video going around claiming certain cities will pay your ENTIRE down payment and let you skip the bank completely. That's not true. But there's something real hiding underneath that myth — three actual cities offering forgivable down payment loans, one covering up to 20% of your purchase price.

South Bend, IN: a forgivable second mortgage equal to 20% of your purchase price. Baltimore, MD: $5,000, but by lottery — only 20 winners a year. Newark, NJ: $20,000 at 0% interest, forgiven over 5 years.

Every program is sourced on screen from its own official page. None of them replace a real mortgage — you still need to qualify with a private lender. That's the honest version, not the viral one.
