Here is the exact automated AI workflow top technical creators are using right now:

Step one is **Vision LLM Hook Auditing**. Before exporting a final video, you run your opening ninety frames through a multimodal vision model like Claude 3.5 Sonnet or GPT-4o Vision using a structured evaluation prompt. 

The prompt instructs the AI to evaluate four specific vectors:
First, **Visual Kinetic Motion**: Is there dynamic physical or graphical transformation occurring within the first four hundred milliseconds, or is the frame static?
Second, **Text Parse Speed**: Can the primary on-screen text be comprehended by the human brain in under six hundred milliseconds?
Third, **The Cognitive Curiosity Gap**: Does the visual frame pose an unanswered high-stakes question without requiring audio context?
And fourth, **Focal Isolation**: Is the primary subject sharply separated from the background noise using high-contrast lighting or isometric framing?
