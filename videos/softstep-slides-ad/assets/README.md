# Drop the client's real assets here

This folder is the ONLY thing the client/agency needs to provide. The whole ad is built
and ready to render around these files — when they land, drop them here with the exact
names below and the final build picks them up automatically.

## Product photos (the most important — 6-10 of them)

One slide per image, clean background, best quality you can get. Straight-on or 3/4
angle. More colors = better.

- `product-1.jpg` ... `product-6.jpg`  (each DIFFERENT slide model/color)

## Lifestyle shots (2-4, optional but strong)

Slides being worn — on feet, walking, styled with an outfit.

- `lifestyle-1.jpg` ... `lifestyle-4.jpg`

## Logo (optional but great)

- `logo.png`  (transparent background if possible)

## Price list (text file — prices WILL be added to the ad)

- `prices.txt` — one line per model, e.g.:  `Men's black slide - GH 150`

## Reviews / customer proof (optional)

- `reviews/` folder — screenshots of comments, DMs, delivery confirmations.
  Only real ones. The ad never invents social proof.

## What the ad currently says (so nothing surprises anyone)

- Name, "step into comfort & style", trendy slides for men & women,
  nationwide delivery, pay on delivery within Accra, DM to order.
- Price slots currently read "DM for price" — they update the moment `prices.txt` lands.
- No invented reviews. No invented prices. That is deliberate: the client asked for
  an ad that converts, and converted ads don't lie on screen.
