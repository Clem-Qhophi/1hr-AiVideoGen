$300 a month can save you $155,461 in mortgage interest and pay your loan off 7.5 years early. Not a trick — real amortization math, run on the actual current mortgage rate, on an ordinary $400,000 loan.

This video breaks down exactly why an extra principal payment does so much more than its own size: how amortization splits every payment between interest and principal, why early payments are mostly interest, and why a dollar paid down early cancels years of interest a dollar paid down late never can.

Two real, computed scenarios: $300 extra a month, and one extra payment a year (the "13th payment" trick) — both run on the same loan, same real current rate, so you can see how the mechanism scales to whatever you can actually afford.

Then the honest part most finance videos skip: when this is NOT automatically the right move — the opportunity cost of not investing that money instead, why an emergency fund comes first, prepayment penalties, and the real value of peace of mind that doesn't show up in the math.

This is general information about how mortgages work, not personalized financial advice. Every number is sourced on screen. Nothing is sold.
