# Rule: 1M-View Viral Packaging & 90+ Score Gate

## Mandatory Packaging Standards
1. **Target**: Every title and thumbnail must be engineered for 1 Million Views.
2. **Multi-Angle Ideation**:
   - Must explore 5 proven viral archetypes:
     1. Superhuman Speed / Superhero Metaphor ("The Flash", "Fastest AI Ever Built")
     2. Competitor Threat / Industry Shock ("RIP Claude?", "Google Ended Sonnet")
     3. The Catch / Pricing Glitch ("50% Price Cut But There's A Catch")
     4. Benchmark Destruction ("65.3% SWE-Bench for $0.75")
     5. Urgent Command ("Do NOT Code Until You Watch This")
3. **Probability Scoring & Gate**:
   - Run `python tools/viral_packaging_engine.py brainstorm videos/<slug> --min-score 90`
   - Must pass the 100-point CTR probability rubric with a score **>= 90/100**.
4. **Thumbnail Aesthetics**:
   - White bold ALL-CAPS font with thick black outline (max 2–4 words).
   - High-contrast visual anchor (split battle, glowing speed gauge, lightning effects).
