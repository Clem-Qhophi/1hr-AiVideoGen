## 3. Channel description (paste into Studio → Customization → Basic info)

1000/1000 characters — YouTube's channel "About" description caps at 1000 (different limit than
video descriptions, which allow 5000).

```
The Closing Table — real estate closing costs, transfer taxes, and the fees nobody explains before you sign, broken down with sourced numbers and the document with your real figure on it.

Every video takes one real, dated number from an industry report and shows the mechanism behind it: why it exists, who pays it, and how much it varies by state and county. Never a national average treated like it applies to you.

Built for first-time buyers, sellers, and refinancers who want to understand their Closing Disclosure before they sign — not after.

No agent pitch. No lender referral. No sponsored recommendation. Just the report, the mechanism, and the document with your number on it.

Covering:
— What's actually inside "closing costs" (lender fees, title insurance, escrow, transfer tax)
— Why the same house costs different amounts in different states
— The questions and documents that catch a bad number before it's too late

Subscribe for the real number and where it came from.
```
